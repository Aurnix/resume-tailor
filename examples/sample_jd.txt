# Example Job Description

**Senior Marketing Operations Manager**
Acme Corp - San Francisco, CA (Remote OK)

## About the Role

We're looking for a Senior Marketing Operations Manager to own our marketing technology stack and drive operational excellence across our growth team.

## Responsibilities

- Own and optimize our marketing technology stack (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.)
- Build and maintain automated workflows and integrations
- Develop dashboards and reporting for marketing performance
- Partner with demand gen, content, and product marketing teams
- Manage vendor relationships and tool evaluations
- Drive process improvements and documentation

## Requirements

- 5+ years in marketing operations or similar role
- Expert-level proficiency with marketing automation platforms
- Strong SQL and data analysis skills
- Experience with Salesforce administration
- Proven track record of building scalable processes
- Excellent cross-functional communication skills

## Preferred

- Experience with Python or JavaScript for automation
- Familiarity with BI tools (Looker, Tableau, etc.)
- Background in B2B SaaS
- Experience managing a team or leading projects

## Benefits

- Competitive salary ($130K-$160K)
- Equity package
- Unlimited PTO
- Remote-first culture
- Health, dental, vision
- 401k match
